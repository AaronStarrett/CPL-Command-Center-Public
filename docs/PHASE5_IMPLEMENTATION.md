## Configuration effects and security boundaries

Company ownership never confers platform authority. Only an independently authorized operator provisions companies and explicitly grants supported modules. Team roles remain a bounded existing role set, with server/database enforcement, reasoned changes, atomic audit and last-active-owner protection. Local synthetic identities use fixed server-owned selections and remain unavailable outside the explicit localhost development boundary. External identity verification and email delivery are not simulated as successful provider actions.

Directory, catalog, branding and template edits affect new work or explicit reviewed draft refreshes. Existing source evidence, approved versions, awarded amount/currency, scheduled UTC instants, exact PDF bytes and delivery recipients remain historical snapshots. Legacy free-text service matching remains intact; a catalog item's fixed workflow key does not change on rename. New templates pin currency. Legacy unpinned templates require explicit currency confirmation when the company default changes; a catalog selection cannot relabel a legacy template's prices, and a template-only override is refused without that template. Company timezone defaults apply when new operational settings are created; saved visit UTC instants are not recalculated.

Company profile and intake policy use the existing versioned organization settings. Required custom intake fields are limited to text, boolean and single-choice values with stable IDs; saved field types cannot change and retirement preserves recorded values. The original readiness minimum remains mandatory, and a changed policy requires current review before proposal preparation. Setup status reads actual saved profile, team/invitations, enabled-module branding/templates, recipes and closeout policies. Optional modules and an intentionally manual workflow do not become fabricated blockers or automatically enabled recipes.

Company administration uses stricter permissions than field-work access. Counts, search, suggestions, audit summaries and downloads must enforce the same live authority as detail/write operations. Tenant and identity context must fence in-flight requests and unsaved forms. Revocation must apply to open sessions and queued work; reactivation cannot revive old invitation or worker grants. No policy setting disables human approval, tenant security or audit requirements.
