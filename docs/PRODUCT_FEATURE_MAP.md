Current product checkpoint: September 23, 2026. Phases 1–3 intake, commercial proposals/projects, execution, field evidence and reports are IMPLEMENTED and WORKING locally. WORKING uses synthetic local identity and records; it does not mean production or full-product acceptance. Physical device/native-download checks and historical deployment receipts retain their separate limits.

The product path is `/workspace` → `/api/cpl`, `/api/cpl-commercial`, `/api/cpl-execution`, `/api/cpl-field`, `/api/cpl-reports`, `/api/cpl-automation` and `/api/cpl-delivery` → tenant-authorized services → organization-scoped PostgreSQL records. Richer legacy screens and engines exist, but their global tables remain test-only. Reuse their validated rules and components through tenant-safe services; do not remove that boundary to expose unfinished features.

Status vocabulary: **IMPLEMENTED**, **WORKING**, **PARTIAL**, **UI ONLY**, **DOMAIN/BACKEND ONLY**, **MOCKED**, **NOT BUILT**, **DEFERRED**. IMPLEMENTED means connected source exists but final milestone verification/acceptance is pending. WORKING requires current execution evidence. PARTIAL identifies a connected subset with missing requested behavior. DOMAIN/BACKEND ONLY describes useful existing logic that is not available in the current tenant product.
