## 4B: work from the Action Center

Use **My actions**, **Reviews**, **Missing information**, **Delivery ready**, **Closeout / invoice issues** and **Automation failures**. Counts come from the authorized company queue; a bounded list is not a claim that the entire company has only that many records.

Select an action to inspect the customer/project, reason, owner, deadline and next action. **Open related record** must open that actual lead, proposal, project, visit, report or package. Save assignment or start/complete/dismiss only when allowed by the server-provided permissions. Completion and dismissal require reasons; neither action fabricates a report approval or delivery.

In **Execution history**, inspect partial success and recorded results before using **Retry unfinished steps**, **Replay recorded event safely**, or **Cancel pending work** with a reason. Replay must reuse existing successful results rather than create a second proposal, project, task or package. Closed source work should reconcile the related action rather than remain a stale review request.

Unsaved forms use **Keep editing** or **Discard changes**. Failed or stale writes retain the entered values. Background queue refresh pauses while editing.

- **Approved artifacts** distinguishes retained approval from a later editable report. Authorized withdrawal requires the exact version, reason and explicit confirmation. It preserves history while making new preparation/sending recheck eligibility.
